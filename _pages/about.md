I'm Thyda Siv, a graduate student in Building Construction and Facility Management at Georgia Institute of Technology. I worked as an  an Architect at The Room Design Studio in Cambodia. The experience has equipped me with the technical and practical skills to manage complex architectural projects from conception to completion. I have supervised multiple architectural projects, including 4 residential projects, a brewery, an office and a data center. Through these experiences, I developed expertise in blueprint reading, reviewing shop drawings and submittals, preparing RFIs, producing schematic designs, design development and construction drawings, creating material specification books, and understanding procedures in construction. My experience also includes coordinating with various stakeholders, resolving design conflicts, and ensuring adherence to specifications and quality standards. 

My passion for building comes from the excitement of witnessing projects evolve from concept to reality. This motivation, combined with my experience collaborating with project architects — where I observed how projects often exceed budgets; and with construction managers during site visits — where I encountered unforeseen construction challenges not evident in design drawings — led me to pursue a master’s degree in Building Construction and Facility Management. 

Currently, through my master’s program at Georgia Tech, I am developing expertise in project management and facility management, while also maintaining a strong focus on building technology and sustainability. I attended an Advanced Project Management course, which I have experience performed quantity take-offs using Bluebeam, and used Excel for cost estimation and preparing change order requests. Through my participation in the Experimental Leadership Program at Georgia Tech, I am familiar with all phases of campus construction projects—from pre-construction through closeout. This included experience with RFQ preparation, contracting methods, payment applications, site logistics, change order, contingency logs, punch lists, and final closeout documentation.

I'm proficient in industry-standard tools including Procore, Bluebeam, SmartSheet, OpenBlue Workplace, CxAlloy , MS Project, Revit, AutoCAD, and MS Excel. Additionally, I am also familiar with laser scanning, 360 imaging, BIM 4D (Asta Powerproject), and Digital Twin (WireTwin and CupixWorks). In addition to my technology expertise, I am passionate about sustainable building practices and hold a LEED Green Associate certification.

When I’m not working, I enjoy practicing yoga, reading, and playing badminton and ping pong. I also love exploring new foods and traveling to experience different cultures and cuisines.
