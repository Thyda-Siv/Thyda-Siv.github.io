<div style="text-align: justify;">
  
I'm Thyda Siv, a graduate student in Building Construction and Facility Management at Georgia Institute of Technology. I hold a Bachelor of Science in Architecture from Zhejiang University in China and worked as an architect at The Room Architecture & Design in Cambodia. The experience has equipped me with the design and technical skills to manage complex architectural projects from conception to completion. I have supervised multiple architectural projects, including an data center + office, a brewery, and residential projects. Through these experiences, I developed expertise in preparing concept designs, design development, and detailed drawings; creating material specification books; reviewing shop drawings and submittals, particularly material samples; organizing BOQs; and understanding design and construction drawings and processes. My experience also includes coordinating with clients, project managers, structural engineers, MEP engineers, interior designers, quantity surveyors, general contractors, suppliers, subcontractors, and consultants. I am advanced in Autodesk Revit, AutoCAD, and MS Excel, and I have skills in Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign, MS Project, SketchUp, Enscape, and Lumion.  

My passion for building comes from the excitement of witnessing projects evolve from concept to reality. This motivation, combined with my experience collaborating with lead architects — where I observed how projects often exceed budgets; and with construction managers during site visits — where I encountered unforeseen construction challenges not seen in detailed drawings — led me to pursue a master’s degree in Building Construction and Facility Management.  

Currently, through my master’s program at Georgia Tech, I am developing expertise in the constructability of projects, spanning the design, construction, and facility management phases. I have gained proficiency with industry-standard tools, including Procore, Bluebeam, Navisworks, Digital Twin (WireTwin, CupixWorks), BIM 4D (Asta Powerproject), Laser Scanning (FARO), and 360 Imaging . In addition, through my participation in the Experimental Leadership Program at Georgia Tech, I am familiar with all phases of campus construction projects—from pre-construction through closeout. This included experience with RFQ preparation, contracting methods, payment applications, site logistics, change order, contingency logs, punch lists, and final closeout documentation. I am also passionate about sustainable building practices and hold a LEED Green Associate certification. Currently, I am supporting a professor at Georgia Tech on a LEED project.  

When I’m not working, I enjoy practicing yoga, reading, and playing badminton and ping pong. I also love exploring new foods and traveling to experience different cultures and cuisines.
